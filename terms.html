Terms & Conditions

These terms apply to SMS messages sent by Tyler Safety Monitor, a personal safety alert program operated by an individual for a trusted caregiver.

Program purpose

Tyler Safety Monitor sends personal safety-related SMS alerts to a trusted caregiver. Alerts may be generated by:

  • a manual emergency or safety trigger;
  • local monitoring software detecting a possible safety issue; or
  • occasional system tests.

These messages are personal safety notifications, not advertising or marketing.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Messages should normally be infrequent and event-driven, with additional occasional test messages used to confirm that the system is functioning.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient’s mobile service plan.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. After opting out, no further Tyler Safety Monitor SMS messages should be sent unless the recipient later provides new consent.

Reply HELP for help.

Consent

Receiving SMS messages is voluntary. Messages are sent only to a trusted caregiver who has explicitly agreed to receive them. SMS consent is not sold or transferred to others.

Emergency-service disclaimer

Tyler Safety Monitor is not a substitute for calling 911 or other emergency services. The system cannot guarantee that a possible safety event will be detected, that an alert will be delivered, or that a recipient will see or act upon an alert.

Do not delay contacting emergency services because of Tyler Safety Monitor. No messaging, hosting, form, software, network, or other service provider guarantees emergency delivery or response.

System availability

The program is a personal project and may be changed, interrupted, or unavailable. Delivery can be affected by power, internet, mobile network, software, device, or third-party service failures.

Privacy

Information handling is described in the Privacy Policy.
