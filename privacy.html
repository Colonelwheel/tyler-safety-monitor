Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled for Tyler Safety Monitor, a personal safety alert project operated by an individual.

Information collected

The system may collect only information needed to operate and troubleshoot personal safety notifications, including:

  • recipient name;
  • recipient mobile phone number;
  • SMS consent status;
  • dates and times associated with consent;
  • alert message content;
  • dates and times alerts are attempted or sent; and
  • limited technical logs necessary to operate and troubleshoot the system.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate Tyler Safety Monitor and send requested personal safety notifications and occasional test messages. It may also be used to maintain the system and investigate delivery or technical problems.

No marketing or sharing of mobile information

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, and messaging consent information will not be shared, sold, rented, or otherwise provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

No information is used for advertising, marketing, lead generation, or promotional campaigns.

Messaging disclosures

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Messages occur only when a safety alert is triggered or during occasional system testing.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe.
  • Reply HELP for help.

Security and retention

Information is limited to what is reasonably necessary for this personal safety system. Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorized access or disclosure. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information may be retained only as long as reasonably needed to operate, document consent for, and troubleshoot the system, subject to the operation of service providers used to send messages or collect the consent form.

Service providers

Tyler Safety Monitor may use service providers strictly to support the requested functions, such as collecting the consent form, hosting this public information, and delivering SMS messages. These services may process limited information as needed to provide their functions. Mobile information is not provided to them for their own marketing or promotional use.

Contact

Questions about this policy or Tyler Safety Monitor may be sent to tyguy9510@hotmail.com.
