Tyler Safety Monitor

Tyler Safety Monitor is a personal safety alert project operated by an individual. It is designed to send SMS notifications to a trusted caregiver when a manual safety alert is triggered or when local monitoring software detects a possible safety concern.

Personal use only. This is not a commercial healthcare service or a substitute for emergency services.
